System Limitations & Important Clarifications

02 / 05
All responses are system-generated by automated logic and are limited to data currently available in ILECO I's live databases. This chatbot interfaces with two separate databases — a Local Operations Database (power outage intake) and a Cloud Incident Database (follow-up, agent queue, meter concerns). Response accuracy depends on the completeness of records in these systems.

Information provided here does not replace, supersede, or constitute official billing statements, formal notices, or Board-approved communications of ILECO I. Concerns involving account disputes, billing corrections, metering irregularities, or legal matters require manual verification by authorized ILECO I personnel. Response timelines for escalated concerns may vary.

Emergency Disclaimer

03 / 05
⚡ Critical Safety Notice — Do Not Delay

In the event of electrical hazards, fallen or downed power lines, transformer explosions, electrical fires, electrocution, or any life-threatening emergency — DO NOT use this chatbot for emergency reporting or response.

Immediately contact the ILECO I 24-Hour Emergency Hotline: 09989893028 or alert the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), Philippine National Police (PNP), or your Local DRRMO. This chatbot operates on automated logic and cannot dispatch emergency crews in real time. Every second matters — please use the hotline.

Data Privacy Compliance

04 / 05
ILECO I is fully compliant with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules under the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

Personal data collected through this chatbot — including your full name, account number, address, contact number, meter details, and concern descriptions — is stored securely in our systems and processed solely for legitimate cooperative service purposes: incident tracking, crew dispatch, agent queue management, and follow-up resolution. Data is never shared with unauthorized third parties.

By continuing to interact with this chatbot, you expressly consent to data collection and processing under our Privacy Policy. To access, correct, or request deletion of your data, contact our Data Protection Officer at any ILECO I office.
